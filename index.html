    What is Cain's Jawbone?

    A murder mystery that is also a puzzle. In 1934 Edward Powys Mathers, who set cryptic crosswords for The Observer as “Torquemada”, published a 100-page novel whose pages were deliberately printed out of order.

    Your task: put the 100 pages back in their correct order, and name the six people who are murdered and the six people who murdered them.

    The story is told through the scattered thoughts of different narrators, and the text is packed with literary quotations, wordplay and 1930s references. Expect to look things up. Only a handful of solvers are known: two in 1935, and the comedian John Finnemore in 2020, after the puzzle was reissued in 2019.

    How to work the case

    • Cabinet: every page, plus folders you create and folders for your tags. Search finds a word on every page.
    • Page reader: highlight words and attach notes, tag the page, and lock it to the page that comes right before or after it.
    • Board: pin pages and sticky notes, and arrange them however helps you think.
    • Solution: put the pages in order and record your verdict on the six murders.
