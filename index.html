    What is Cain's Jawbone?

    A 1934 murder mystery by Edward Powys Mathers (“Torquemada”, The Observer's cryptic crossword setter), with its 100 pages deliberately printed out of order.

    Your task: put the pages back in order, and name the six people murdered and the six people who murdered them.

    Expect several narrators, literary quotations, wordplay and 1930s references. Only a handful of people are known to have solved it: two in 1935, and John Finnemore in 2020.

    How to work the case

    • Cabinet: every page, your folders, tag folders, and search.
    • Page reader: highlight and annotate words, tag pages, lock a page to the one before or after it.
    • Board: pin pages and sticky notes anywhere.
    • Solution: put the pages in order and record your verdict.
